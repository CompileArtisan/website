# Climbing up the lighthouse

We&rsquo;re all in a journey to keep rising higher, the skies and the heaven beyond.
The height you&rsquo;re at could be a measure of success, a measure of time, or maybe
even a measure of happiness. Different people have different ways to climb up
this *lighthouse of heights*, and they can choose how much of it they want to
reveal to everyone else. Some climb up the staircase, while the others might fly
with their wings. Some may keep all the windows shut tight keeping the
lighthouse insulated from the rest of the world, while the others might want the
windows wide open, for the whole world to know. You are your mind and your
emotions, while the lighthouse symbolizes your physical self. 

One particular boy ascends heights using the staircase, although quite
erratically. He started with an environment where he was naturally more capable
than everyone to rise. He looks outside his window to interact with the rest of
the world and at this point of time, everyone outside his window looked upto
him.
